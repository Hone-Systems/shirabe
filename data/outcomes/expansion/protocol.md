Keep the original 20-question rubric. A replication outcome concerns a specified result, not every claim or scientific utility. A failed replication is not automatically a disproof or absence of later replication; distinguish bounded negative findings from universal negatives and unresolved mixed evidence. Preserve unknown and N/A masks. Research through 2026-09-16 and record source publication dates. Independent label checks precede fitting; assess known-answer agreement and missingness disagreements separately. Label checking is independent judgment, not ground truth.

Fit the pinned pretrained BERT-Mini to per-question targets using all available original-text tokens in nonoverlapping 512-token chunks, equal paper weights, masked BCE. Wording topic redaction receives source text only. Compare wording, original and topic-only variants. Training fractions 1/3, 2/3 and all use nested deterministic paper samples and validation curves; full-data fits use seeds 42,43,44. Eight epochs, fixed AdamW learning rate3e-5, validation-loss checkpoint selection. No hyperparameter search against test or challenge outcomes.

Report train-only smoothed per-head prevalence and a regularized rhetorical-feature baseline; compare metadata-only controls (publication year, field, cohort and input scope). Main metrics: paper-weighted Brier/log loss, macro per-head results where both outcomes occur, and paper-cluster bootstrap intervals for Brier improvement against prevalence. Report per-dimension and per-cohort coverage; do not pool correlated answers as independent samples. AUROC only on heads with both classes, with paper counts. A held-out score is not an overall paper success percentage.

A promising signal requires consistent seed behavior, improvement over controls on fresh held-out papers, and uncertainty small enough to support that improvement. Also examine validation learning curves and cohort transfer/topic leakage. If these checks fail, report failure without changing labels or choosing a favorable metric. More training cannot guarantee the hypothesis is true. A credible null or inconclusive result is useful; do not train indefinitely to obtain a desired number.

RL remains conditional: no RL until contrasting independently supported rewards exist and the supervised study establishes a meaningful held-out signal. Additional fixed-label RL is not new evidence. No deployment based solely on reduced training loss.
