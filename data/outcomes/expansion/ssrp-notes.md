# SSRP expansion audit

All 21 original papers enumerated in references 16–36 of Camerer et al. (2018) were included; no original was selected based on its replication result. DOI-derived splits are recorded in `ssrp-manifest.json`. The aggregate replication rate and incidental supplement outcomes were visible during cohort discovery; the manifest makes that exposure explicit. The cohort is complete relative to SSRP eligibility criteria, not representative of all scientific papers. All originals were published in Nature or Science in 2010–2015 and reported selected significant experimental effects.

All 21 identities were verified against original abstracts. Six original author-body texts were downloaded and read, with 15 assessments explicitly abstract-only. Private text files retain complete available abstracts/body; identified publisher material and references were removed without length caps. Exact author-manuscript/proof equivalence to the final publication was not independently audited. The private input map records source, scope, hash and processing.

The 420 rubric answers contain 75 yes, 5 no and 340 unknown. Three training negatives, one validation negative and one calibration negative all concern stronger tests. There are no negatives in the two-paper test subset. Consequently this cohort alone does not supply a balanced paper-level success benchmark, and the 20 answers from a paper are not independent observations.

A direct replication is evidence about its selected contrast. It was not automatically counted as scientific uptake, product adoption, substantive reuse, utility or lasting consensus. In particular:

- Kovács's reaction-time contrast reproduces, but independent artifact-control experiments challenge the automatic-belief interpretation.
- Shah's failed cognitive-fatigue experiment is distinct from the central overborrowing claim.
- Sparrow's failed computer-word Stroop contrast does not settle its memory-location claim.
- Kidd has contradictory independent replication outcomes and analysis-dependent findings.
- Lee's null replication has an acknowledged album-order deviation whose effect remains unresolved.
- Ramirez's pressure manipulation was disputed; the negative answer is limited to the tested laboratory contrast.
- Nishi's August 2018 replication is slightly short of three full years after September 2015, so it does not satisfy the late-positive-assessment threshold.

All binary answers cite read primary evidence or a clearly identified research synthesis. These grades are evidence-bounded, not exhaustive lifetime-impact assessments. Missing application evidence stays unknown. The shared replication paper creates correlated source coverage and label missingness. Independent grading agreement and validation controls remain necessary before interpreting model results.
